Terms of Service & UK GDPR Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: October 2026 • Aligned with UK Data Protection Act 2018, UK GDPR, EU AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689 Annex III), UK Equality Act 2010, & ISO/IEC 42001:2023

Please read these Terms of Service and Data Governance provisions carefully before using PostYourJobUK. By registering a recruiter workspace, posting a job specification, or submitting a candidate application, you agree to be bound by the following terms.

1. Acceptance of Terms & Eligibility

By accessing or using any part of the PostYourJobUK platform, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms of Service. Recruiter workspaces are restricted to authorised hiring managers, HR professionals, and registered organisations (18+).

2. Multi-Tenant Isolation & BOLA Security

Each recruiter organisation operates within a strictly isolated tenant boundary (tenant_id). Attempting to access, modify, or exfiltrate job postings or candidate CVs belonging to another tenant is blocked by Broken Object Level Authorization (BOLA) guards and logged in system_logs.

3. Stage 0 PII Redaction & Data Minimization

In compliance with UK GDPR Article 5(1)(c) (Data Minimization), candidate CVs undergo automated Stage 0 regular-expression anonymization prior to AI inference—redacting names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, graduation years, and demographic markers before text reaches Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.

4. Encrypted Cloud Storage & Right to Erasure

Uploaded PDF and DOCX resumes are stored in encrypted Azure Blob Storage (hranalyticalstorage) scoped strictly to the hiring job and tenant. Recruiters can permanently delete individual or bulk candidate records and cloud CV blobs at any time in accordance with UK GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure).

5. Algorithmic Transparency (EU AI Act Annex III)

AI compatibility scores are advisory decision-support metrics computed deterministically from verbatim CV evidence and Base-100 subtractive math. Every evaluation generates an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic audit fingerprint (ai_audit_fingerprint), and final hiring decisions remain under human recruiter oversight.

6. Non-Discrimination & UK Equality Act 2010

Recruiters must ensure all job specifications comply with the UK Equality Act 2010 and do not discriminate across protected characteristics. PostYourJobUK reserves the right to suspend any tenant account (status = 'suspended') that posts unlawful, misleading, or discriminatory listings.

7. Transactional Email Communications

By submitting an application or advancing a candidate in the ATS pipeline, users consent to receive transactional status notifications (Application Confirmation, Interview Invitation, and Formal Job Offer) dispatched via our authenticated Brevo SMTP relay (careers@postyourjobuk.co.uk).

8. Client-Side Resume Builder Privacy

The Free ATS CV & Resume Builder (/resume-builder) executes 100% client-side inside the user's browser. Draft resume inputs are never transmitted to or stored on PostYourJobUK servers unless the candidate subsequently chooses to upload the generated PDF to an active job listing.

9. Limitation of Liability & Governing Law (England & Wales)

These Terms of Service are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes arising in connection with the platform shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
