🚀 Speed Without Compromise

Post a live job specification in minutes and receive instant AI-scored candidate evaluations asynchronously via Azure Storage Queues—removing manual screening bottlenecks from sign-up to shortlisting.

🔎 Verbatim Evidence Over Formatting

Our Python Grounding Gate rejects unverified AI claims and job-description copy-pasting (difflib > 0.85), ensuring recruiters only see candidate scores backed by exact character-for-character quotes from the CV.

⚖️ Mathematical Parity by Design

By stripping names, contact handles, birth dates, graduation eras, and affinity markers before LLM extraction and applying deterministic Base-100 subtractive math, identical qualifications always yield an identical score (0.0 pt delta).

🤝 Respectful Candidate Communication

Candidates are never left in the dark. Every application triggers an instant HTML confirmation receipt, and 1-click recruiter stage transitions dispatch official Interview Invitations and Job Offers with direct hiring contact details.

🇬🇧 UK-Rooted, Regulatory-First Engineering

Purpose-built for UK organisations with native alignment to the UK Equality Act 2010 (Section 19), UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018, EU AI Act (Annex III), and ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

♿ Universal Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 Level AA)

Engineered with high-contrast dark-mode typography, keyboard-navigable tables and focus-trapped modals, screen-reader live regions, and a free client-side ATS CV builder so every job seeker can participate equally.
